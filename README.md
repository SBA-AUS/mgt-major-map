## What it is

An interactive, one-page guide for students in the Bachelor of Science in Business Administration (BSBA), Major in Management. It brings together the four-year degree plan, out-of-class experiences and career outcomes.

- **Your Proposed Four-Year Journey:** courses by semester, milestones (including the BUS 397 internship), and year-by-year activities under Learn, Lead & Connect, Explore and Launch, plus a checklist for each year.
- **Career Paths:** four paths (Entrepreneurship & Family Business, Consulting & Business Development, Human Resource Management, Project Management), each with a core course, core/elective courses and alternatives.
- **Where It Leads:** skills mapped to the WEF *Future of Jobs Report 2025* and the NACE career readiness competencies, careers, employers, and graduate study (MBA and PhD in Management).
- **Course details:** click any course to see its catalog description, prerequisites and related approved Coursera courses.
- **Coursera:** approved Coursera courses appear as blue icons on the skills cards and inside the course details. Hover for details, click to open.

Always confirm requirements in the current [AUS Undergraduate Catalog](https://www.aus.edu/resources/undergraduate-catalog-2026-2027).

## Updating the map

1. Replace `index.html` with the new version of the map.
2. Add a copy of the new version to `versions/` (for example `AUS_Management_Major_Map_v1.14.html`).
3. Add a row to the version history above.

GitHub Pages republishes the site automatically within a minute or two of each change.
